TaxID is the NCBI Taxonomy identifier. Richness counts distinct assigned taxa, not species — a genus and its species are counted separately, so Gobiomorphus and Gobiomorphus cotidianus both contribute.

Interpolated surfaces use inverse-distance weighting from binned sample means, and fade out beyond 0.45° from any sample, so coverage follows where people have actually sampled. They are a reading aid, not a modelled prediction.

All parsing happens locally with PapaParse streaming; nothing leaves the machine.
